Why It Matters
Your first meeting sets the rhythm.
Whatever format you establish here becomes the seed Accord carries forward to every follow-up. Pick a template that matches the meeting's nature — or start blank if it's truly bespoke.
How Accord Helps
After today, this column fills with intelligence.
Open commitments by attendee. Decisions awaiting belief. Comments from people who read the minutes. Substrate momentum since you last met. Right now there's no history — but Accord is about to start building it.
The Discipline
Every thread terminates somewhere.
Each agenda item must end in a decision, an assigned action, a recorded risk, a posed question — or an explicit deferral. Discussion-without-commitment is structurally eliminated.

Composing the Agenda
your meeting's structure
The Composition Surface
This is where your agenda comes alive.
Each agenda item is a thread. Every thread will produce substrate during the meeting — a decision, an action, a risk, a question. Type freely; Accord infers the NRA shape from your language.
Async Collaboration
Attendees can suggest items before you begin.
Once you send the invite, attendees see the draft agenda. They can suggest threads to add, comment on existing items, or pre-declare beliefs on pending decisions. Their input arrives here as you prep.
References & Canvas
Documents and visuals carry forward.
Attached canvas images, PDFs, spreadsheets, build packages — they persist across meetings in this workstream. At setup you decide what's still relevant; pruning happens here, not in some buried settings page.

Time Pressure
Accord learns your meeting rhythm.
After a few meetings in this workstream, Accord knows how long similar meetings actually take. If you've scheduled 30 minutes for what historically runs 47, the column will warn you. Trim the agenda or extend the slot.
